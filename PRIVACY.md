# Club Mode Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Club Mode is an independent project published by Tom Johnson under the name ARCHERY.

Contact: enquiries@archery-dj.com  

Club Mode works locally on your Mac. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and ARCHERY does not receive information about how you use the app.

Club Mode uses the following Mac permissions:

- **Accessibility** to read rekordbox interface labels and positions
- **Screen Recording** to process the rekordbox window for split waveforms and phase meters

Screen contents are processed temporarily in memory. They are not saved, transmitted or shared. Club Mode does not capture audio or control rekordbox.

Settings are stored locally. **Copy Diagnostic Data** places technical information on your clipboard but sends nothing automatically. It does not include screenshots, audio, music files or track titles.

External links, including GitHub, Instagram and Ko-fi, are governed by those services’ privacy policies.

If you contact ARCHERY for support, the information you provide will be used only to handle your request. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact address above.
